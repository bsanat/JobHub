# Provider permissions checklist

Use this checklist before moving an imported vacancy source beyond proof of
concept.

## Evidence to collect

- Storage rights for vacancy fields used by JobHub.
- Display and redistribution rights for public pages.
- Attribution requirements and required link behavior.
- Retention, removal and deletion obligations.
- Whether complete snapshots, deltas or ranked partial searches are provided.
- Lifecycle signals for archived, closed, expired or removed vacancies.
- Rate limits and request-budget expectations.

## JobHub gates

- Keep unapproved sources hidden in production.
- Keep external applications on provider or employer links.
- Do not invent company identity, salary, publication date or lifecycle state.
- Keep source provenance visible in internal data.

Jooble remains proof of concept until its production permissions are confirmed.
